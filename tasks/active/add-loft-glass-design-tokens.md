---
title: Add the Loft's glass design tokens
created: 2026-10-28
---

Give the Loft its new look in `packages/ui`. Remap the shadcn tokens to the dark glass palette, add the `glass` and `lamp` utilities, and self-host Archivo, Instrument Serif and JetBrains Mono. Add `glass` and `lamp` Button variants with `pill` and `mic` sizes, plus the reduced-transparency and reduced-motion fallbacks.

Done when the existing Loft screens render with the new tokens and fonts, and no text on glass falls below 4.5:1 contrast.

Related: [Loft design spec §4](../../docs/design/loft.md#4-visual-language)
