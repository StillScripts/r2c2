Design the full sign-up and sign-in experience on top of the MVP's email and password ([ADR-009](../../docs/decisions/ADR-009-email-password-sign-in-for-mvp.md)).

- Google, GitHub and possibly Facebook as Better Auth social providers
- Email verification and password reset through Resend (after T20)
- Possibly magic links
- One polished sign-up/sign-in form that covers all of the above
