---
title: Make the Loft work on phones
created: 2026-10-28
---

Build the phone screens: home, conversation, voice and the publish sheet. The input sits at the bottom within thumb reach, and the view has no window frame. Make the Loft installable as a web app, and send a notification when a draft is ready to publish or new enquiries arrive.

Done when screens D to G work at 390px wide and a notification opens the right screen.
